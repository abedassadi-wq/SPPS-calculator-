Calculation basis
Scale (mmol) = Initial dry resin (g) × Loading (mmol/g)
Initial dry resin (g) = Scale (mmol) ÷ Loading (mmol/g)
Theoretical peptide–resin (g) = Initial dry resin (g) + Scale (mmol) × [Protected peptide MW + Resin MW correction] ÷ 1,000
Expected final peptide (g) = Scale (mmol) × Unprotected peptide MW (g/mol) × Process yield (%) ÷ 100,000
Chemistry note: The −222 g/mol Fmoc correction is included as requested for Fmoc-bearing resins. Its validity depends on whether the supplied protected-peptide MW includes the initial Fmoc contribution and how the resin-bound linker and peptide termini are represented. Do not apply Fmoc subtraction twice. For Wang, 2-CTC and custom resins, the default correction is zero pending a defined linkage mass balance. Theoretical peptide–resin assumes full-length conversion of every loaded site and excludes moisture, solvent and truncated chains.

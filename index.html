Calculation basis
Scale (mmol) = Initial dry resin (g) × Loading (mmol/g)
Initial dry resin (g) = Scale (mmol) ÷ Loading (mmol/g)
Theoretical peptide–resin (g) = Initial dry resin (g) + Scale (mmol) × [Protected peptide MW + Resin MW correction] ÷ 1,000
Expected final peptide (g) = Scale (mmol) × Unprotected peptide MW (g/mol) × Process yield (%) ÷ 100,000
